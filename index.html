About me

Hi, I'm Siqi Lu! I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at The College of William & Mary, working in the Robust and Adaptive Intelligence Laboratory (RAIL) under Dr. Ayan Mukhopadhyay. I hold an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Vanderbilt University and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

My research focuses on decision making under uncertainty — building AI systems that reason robustly and transparently in complex, real-world environments. I'm also interested in explainable AI and have prior research experience in biomedical imaging and remote sensing.

Research Areas

  • Decision Making Under Uncertainty

    Building robust AI systems that reason and plan under uncertainty, including explainability frameworks for Monte Carlo Tree Search.

  • Explainable AI

    Developing interpretable and transparent AI systems, including LLM-based pipelines for generating evidence-grounded explanations.

  • Biomedical Imaging

    Nuclei segmentation and AI foundation model evaluation in kidney pathology at Vanderbilt University Medical Center.

  • Remote Sensing

    Survey and evaluation of 59 vision foundation models for remote sensing; active learning for agriculture segmentation.
